# Document Drafting

Identify the intended reader, deliverable format, source material, and requested change. Preserve supplied facts and distinguish missing information from editorial decisions. When editing, inspect the original structure and avoid discarding unrelated content.

Build the document around the reader's decisions. Track quotations and quantitative claims to their sources, and mark unsupported placeholders explicitly. For DOCX/PDF or other binary formats, inspect available libraries and rendering tools rather than treating plain text as a formatted artifact.

Reopen the saved artifact to confirm its contents. If layout matters and rendering is available, inspect the rendered pages for clipping, broken tables, and headings stranded from their content. Report format or visual checks that could not be performed.
