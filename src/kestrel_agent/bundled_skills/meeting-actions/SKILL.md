---
name: meeting-actions
description: Extract decisions, owners, deadlines, and unresolved questions from meeting notes or transcripts.
---

Read the supplied notes/transcript and preserve speaker attribution when reliable. Separate actual decisions from suggestions and unresolved proposals. Extract each action with its stated owner and deadline; use null or an explicit unknown marker when absent rather than inventing assignments or dates. Distinguish relative dates from resolved calendar dates unless the meeting date is known. Identify contradictory commitments and source locations. Produce the user's requested table or machine-readable format. Do not send messages, create calendar events, or assign external tasks merely because the notes mention them.
